Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

1. Introduction

StoryNest is committed to protecting the privacy of users and their families. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and how we safeguard it when you use the StoryNest platform.

2. Information We Collect

We may collect the following information:

  • Name and account details.
  • Email address and login credentials.
  • Date of birth for age-appropriate content recommendations.
  • Reading preferences and activity history.
  • Content saved to favourites or watchlists.
  • Device and usage information required for platform functionality.

3. How We Use Your Information

Your information is used to:

  • Create and manage your account.
  • Provide personalized reading recommendations.
  • Track reading progress and achievements.
  • Enable parental controls and account management.
  • Improve platform performance and user experience.

4. Children's Privacy

StoryNest provides features for children under parental supervision. Child accounts are managed by a parent or guardian, who may control content access, viewing permissions, and account settings.

5. Data Sharing

StoryNest does not sell personal information. Information is only shared when required by law or when necessary to provide services that support the operation of the platform.

6. Data Security

We implement reasonable security measures to protect personal data from unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration, or destruction.

7. Your Rights

Users may update their profile information, change passwords, and request account deletion through the account management features provided within StoryNest.

8. Contact Us

If you have questions regarding this Privacy Policy, please contact the StoryNest support team.
