About StoryNest

A reading library built for children, run by the grown-ups who care for them.

What it is

StoryNest is an interactive library of books and videos for children. Young readers browse a shelf that has already been filtered for them, keep favourites and a watchlist, track what they have finished, earn badges, and ask the Story Chat for something new to read.

Everything a child can see is decided by the adult who looks after their account — never by the child, and never by chance.

Who uses it

AccountWhat they can do
Guest Browse the library and read title pages. Saving, progress and Story Chat need an account.
Child Their own account details and the part of the library their grown-up allows. Favourites, watchlist, reading progress, badges, reviews and Story Chat. They can ask for a locked title to be unlocked.
Adult Their own account, plus every child account they create: age limits, blocked genres, daily screen time, unlock requests, and a record of what each child has been doing.
Administrator Every account on the site, the whole catalogue, reported content, announcements and the full audit trail.

How we keep it safe

  • Filtering happens on the server. A blocked title is never sent to a child's browser, so it cannot be found by poking around.
  • Children sign in with a short login ID chosen by their parent — no email address required.
  • Passwords are stored as bcrypt hashes; the session cookie cannot be read by page scripts.
  • Every meaningful action is written to an audit log that parents and administrators can read.
